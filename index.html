02 · What the six weeks say

Six findings worth acting on

Finding 01 · Confirmed, and it worked

The reporting gap was a missing spec, and writing one fixed it

The 15.09 edition argued the gap was a missing standard, not missing discipline. Nine days later the rate has gone from 41% to 84% and three people have perfect records. The two largest rises — Simge 36→100% and Başak 24→71% — belong to the two people who were given something concrete: a footfall report Simge can read, and a handover that took the ambiguity out of Başak's scope. Onur, added on 16.09 with no format brief, is the one person still filing in a shape nobody can read. The pattern holds in both directions.

Finding 02 · The big one, unchanged

Stock truth is still broken, and four people are still hand-patching it

Nothing in the last nine days has touched the root cause. Başak has been waiting on Biricik Hanım's stock list since 10.09 — chased 18.09 and 21.09, still open 23.09 — and the Aydınlatma V2 campaign is queued behind it. Aysar cannot enter stock quantities at all: "Design Shop olarak stok adetleriyle oynamak gibi bir yetkimiz şu an bulunmuyor (sistem bize kapalı)." Betül spent 21.09 talking to online customers about stock errors and returns. Simge re-sent a website list on 16.09 that had sat unactioned since 9 Eylül. One defect, four mailboxes, six weeks.

Finding 03 · New · Revenue blocked at the till

The payment form has been broken for at least four days and nobody owns it

Betül hit it on 19.09 and routed it to Rodin, who identified iyzico. Başak raised it again on 21.09 and it was still open on 23.09, with her note "müşteriler arıyor". Separately Aysar reports a 30.000₺ per-invoice cap pushing customers to IBAN transfer and creating manual invoicing loops. This is not an analytics problem or a campaign problem — customers who decided to buy cannot pay. It sits between two vendors and no one inside the building is named on it.

Finding 04 · New · The cost of a brief

One creative piece was built six times because nobody would sign it off

Zeynep B. rebuilt the Eames Roadshow mailing four times and reached V6 by 23.09. She named the fix herself on 19.09 — shorter, finalised briefs — and repeated it as a request, not an excuse. The same week, her Canva V1 and her QR package both stalled waiting on Ceren, and the QR question closed with "Balamire soralım" and no answer. She is the most disciplined reporter on the team and the one losing the most time to other people's decisions.

Finding 05 · Escalated

Two of your three top sellers are spending their week on admin and one is now on a building fault

Aysar closed 352.255₺ on 24.09 alone — the largest single day in the record. The same report lists sweeping the shop, watering the plants, updating the inventory spreadsheet, and a two-page account of an electrical fault under the outdoor planters with a flood risk, an unfitted pump motor and a key held by the landlord's assistant. Esin's 16.09 escalation on invoicing, cash sales and returns is still unanswered nine days later. Two decisions from you — the Paola C discount and the Ortaköy access question — are both sitting in the 24.09 report.

Finding 06 · Correction and carry-over

What I got wrong last time, and what is still true

The 15.09 edition listed İrem Beliz Öztürk among the eight; she has left the company and her online scope went to marketing, so the list is now Betül, Ceren, Esin, Aysar, Simge, Zeynep B., Başak and Onur. 03 · The actual ask

Thirteen things a machine should be doing

Ordered by estimated hours returned per month; sizing unchanged from the 15.09 read. Status flags reflect what moved in the last nine days.

Online support & info-form triage
~25 h/mo

Now ~11 enquiries a day and rising — 17 on 21.09. Almost all are stock, price, delivery or lead-time questions on catalogue items. An auto-classifier with a templated first response, pulling live product data, handles the majority. Still the single largest recurring block on the team.

DO FİRSTBetül
Stock reconciliation: warehouse vs online vs Logo
~20 h/mo

One root cause, four people patching it by hand. Unmoved in nine days: Başak is still waiting on a list requested 10.09, Aysar still has no write access. A scheduled diff report replaces all of it and closes the oversell risk.

DO FİRSTBaşak, Aysar, Simge, Betül
Payment-failure alerting & ownership
revenue, not hours

New on this list. The iyzico form has been failing since at least 19.09 with no single owner; it surfaced twice, in two mailboxes, four days apart. A monitored checkout with an alert and a named owner turns a four-day outage into a one-hour one.

DO FİRSTBaşak + vendor
Stock & availability answers on WhatsApp / phone
10–15 h/mo

Described as a daily interrupt. A queryable stock-and-price lookup, or a WhatsApp auto-reply on SKU, removes the biggest single interruption in her day.

DO FİRSTSimge
Product listing & image preparation
1–2 days/mo

Başak now times it: ~25 minutes an item, up to an hour for someone who doesn't know the range. A managed asset library plus bulk CSV listing and a rule-based markup removes nearly all of it. Currently being outsourced instead — which prices the problem rather than solving it.

DO FİRSTBetül, Zeynep B.
Logo ERP price, VAT & material-slip entry
12–20 h/mo

Four times a week, pure rule-based transfer from supplier price lists. A price-list import template using the markup formula she already defined removes most of it. Blocked further by her having no write access to quantities.

DO FİRSTAysar
Contact-list building & audience segmentation
4–6 h/campaign

Export → normalise → dedupe → consent-flag → size-check → push to Google/Meta. Was a hard blocker on every paid campaign; the file she was given held 53 addresses against a 100 minimum.

DO FİRSTBaşak
Hand-built Excel lists
12–16 h/mo

Vitrine list, wall list, storage addressing, product codes, shoot lists, stock orders — every one an extract of data that already exists in Hillpos. One parameterised report replaces the lot.

Half solved. Footfall data became available on 21.09 and Simge now states a customer count daily — but the visitor is still logged in Excel, the Public file and the website, and every sale entered twice. One form feeding all sinks finishes the job.
