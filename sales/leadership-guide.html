Key Insights

Retail dominates at 55% of total revenue. 5 of the top 8 reps are in Retail. Two new Retail reps join the roster: Deniz Boyraz (€6.9k across 3 deals in his first month) and Öykü Öner (no figures filed in the sheet yet).
Zeynep is the clear #1 in revenue, leading by +40% over the next rep — İdil holds #2, with Burçin and Sena now within €40k of each other at #3 and #4.
August 2026: €2.14M — down −48% on July and −16% against August 2025 (€2.54M). August is seasonally the weakest month, but this is the softest August in the series. 2026 YTD stands at €29.80M against the €33.0M full-year target, i.e. +35% ahead of a pro-rated 8-month plan. Caveat: Banu, Derin and Zeynep filed partial August detail, and Beliz’s €45 across 9 deals looks like a data-entry error — the month may firm up as the sheet is completed.
Volume vs. Value gap: Ceren M. leads in units sold (778) but ranks 18th in revenue — high-volume, low-ticket. Betül shows the pattern more sharply: 200 units in August alone, worth €552k, taking her to #2 on units all-time.
Lighting is a growth opportunity at 13% share with strong volume from Biricik (518 units) — its customer counts are built up from rep-level records (803 customers, 28% new).

How to Use the Dashboard

Filters

Toggle between Revenue and Units Sold at the top, and switch the currency between € EUR and ₺ TRY (converted at the live daily rate). Filter by time: All Time, FY 2025, FY 2026 YTD, Q1 2026, Last 6 months, or a custom month range.

Tabs

Overview for KPIs and trends. Departments for dept-level drill-down. Sales Reps for individual performance. Customers for client-level data. Deep Analysis for cross-cutting insights.

Drill-Down

Click any department, rep name, or table row for detailed breakdowns.
