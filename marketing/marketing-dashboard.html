How to read the pulse row

Read it left to right. Demand → Engagement → Money → Efficiency → Brand. Start at Revenue: if it moved, walk right to find out whether it was a traffic problem, a conversion problem or an efficiency problem. Those are three different fixes and three different owners.

The row is fixed to the last complete month, so the number is stable. The date filter at the top drives the detail below, not this row.

  • 1 Revenue — total store revenue from Metorik. The sub-line is the share Meta's pixel claims; the pixel over-credits, so read it as an upper bound.
  • 2 Sessions — GA4, mozaikdesign.com, Türkiye-only.
  • 3 Conversion — online orders ÷ sessions, from raw counts. Not GA4's own conversion rate, which is void while view_item is flagged as a key event. Showroom closes are not in here.
  • 4 Meta ROAS — Meta pixel revenue ÷ Meta spend. The spend figure beside it is Meta + Google combined.
  • 5 Brand Reach — Instagram organic + paid. A leading indicator: next quarter's demand, not this one's.

Mozaik Outlet is a catalogue, not a shop · what this tab measures

Its job is to put the range in front of people and hand warm interest to the sales team — so there is deliberately no basket, no checkout and no revenue on this tab. Success here means: how many people saw the catalogue, how far through it they got, and how many asked to be contacted.
