How each tile is built

Revenue — total online store revenue from Metorik. The sub-line is the share Meta's pixel claims; the pixel over-credits, so read it as an upper bound.

Sessions — GA4, mozaikdesign.com, Türkiye-only (bot traffic from other countries excluded).

Conversion — online orders ÷ sessions from raw counts, not GA4's own rate, which is void while view_item is flagged as a key event. Showroom closes are not in here.

Meta ROAS — Meta pixel revenue ÷ Meta spend. The spend beside it is Meta + Google combined.

Brand reach — Instagram organic + paid. A leading indicator: next quarter's demand, not this one's.

The Pulse is fixed to the last complete month so the number is stable. The date filter drives the other tabs.

Verdict rules

Scale ROAS ≥ 4x and under 30% of spend. Hold 2–4x. Cut under 1x with more than ₺20k spend in each of the last two months. Review traffic / awareness campaigns (judged on hand-offs, not ROAS) and any row with ₺0 tracked revenue against real spend — usually a tagging problem, not a performance one.
